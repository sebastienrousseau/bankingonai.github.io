# Privacy Policy

We take data privacy and confidentiality seriously. Banking On AI operates under strict privacy-by-design principles.

## Data Collection & Usage

- **Zero Tracking Cookies**: We do not use third-party behavioral tracking cookies or external ad trackers.
- **Contact Submissions**: Information submitted through contact forms is utilized solely to respond to specific advisory inquiries.
- **Data Retention**: Personal contact information is securely stored and never shared with third parties.
