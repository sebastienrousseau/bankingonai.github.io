# Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Banking On AI address regulatory compliance?
Every architectural pattern aligns directly with established regulatory guidelines including Federal Reserve SR 11-7, OCC Model Risk Management Guidance, Basel Committee principles on operational resilience, and the EU AI Act.

### Can these models be deployed on-premises?
Yes. All architectures support air-gapped on-premises deployment, private cloud Kubernetes clusters, and dedicated VPC enclaves ensuring zero external data leakage.

### How are algorithmic bias and fairness verified?
We utilize statistical parity testing, equalized odds metrics, and counterfactual fairness assessments across all credit scoring and automated underwriting pipelines.
