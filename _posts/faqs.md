# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about artificial intelligence implementation in modern financial institutions.

## General Questions

### What is Banking On AI?
Banking On AI is an open research and technical intelligence platform exploring the intersection of advanced machine learning, regulatory governance, and financial systems architecture.

### How does AI comply with strict financial regulations?
All AI architectures showcased adhere to strict model risk management standards (such as Federal Reserve SR 11-7, OCC guidance, and the EU AI Act), prioritizing explainability, deterministic fallback controls, and immutable audit logs.

### Are open-source models suitable for core banking?
Open-source foundation models fine-tuned within private, VPC-isolated environments allow financial institutions to maintain strict data sovereignty while eliminating third-party API exposure.
