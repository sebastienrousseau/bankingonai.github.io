# AI Solutions by Financial Sector

Explore targeted applications of artificial intelligence across specialized banking divisions.

## 1. Retail & Consumer Banking
- **Intelligent Spending Insights**: Real-time budget forecasting and proactive liquidity nudges.
- **24/7 Contextual Assistants**: Autonomous resolution of account servicing, card security, and dispute filing.
- **Fair Lending Verification**: Continuous algorithmic fairness auditing across demographic cohorts.

## 2. Commercial Lending & Credit Analysis
- **Automated Financial Spreading**: Extract and validate balance sheets, tax returns, and cash-flow statements from unstructured PDFs in seconds.
- **Dynamic Risk Rating**: Continuous macro-economic scenario simulation across commercial debt portfolios.

## 3. Corporate Treasury & Payments
- **Intelligent Payment Routing**: Predictive routing of high-value cross-border wires to minimize correspondent banking fees and FX slippage.
- **Working Capital Optimization**: Automated cash-sweep algorithms optimizing yield across global accounts.

## 4. Capital Markets & Trading
- **Signal Extraction**: Natural language processing across global filings, earnings calls, and news feeds.
- **Trade Surveillance**: Real-time anomaly detection identifying spoofing, front-running, and unauthorized risk limit breaches.
