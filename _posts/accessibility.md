# Accessibility Statement

Banking On AI is engineered to provide universal access to all visitors in accordance with **WCAG 2.2 Level AAA** standards.

## Accessibility Principles

- **High Contrast Palettes**: Color pairs validated with mathematical contrast ratios exceeding 7.0:1 for standard text and 4.5:1 for headings.
- **Keyboard Operability**: Full navigation capability via keyboard with clear, high-visibility focus indicators.
- **Assistive Technology Optimization**: Strict semantic hierarchy, descriptive alt tags, and ARIA labels.
- **Reduced Motion**: Honoring `prefers-reduced-motion` settings across all interactive elements.
