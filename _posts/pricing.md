# Advisory & Technical Engagement

We partner with banks, fintech innovators, and regulatory authorities to design, evaluate, and scale production-grade AI systems.

## Engagement Models

- **Open Research Tier**: Full access to published architectural whitepapers, benchmark data, and open-source validation scripts.
- **Technical Architecture Review**: Comprehensive 4-week architectural evaluation of internal AI pipelines against global model risk governance standards.
- **Enterprise Pilot & Co-Development**: Dedicated engineering sprints for high-impact use cases (real-time fraud detection, IFRS LLM advisory, credit underwriting).
