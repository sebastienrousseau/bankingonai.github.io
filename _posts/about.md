# About Banking On AI

Banking On AI was established to bridge the critical gap between cutting-edge machine learning innovation and the rigorous safety, governance, and auditability standards demanded by global financial regulators.

## Our Philosophy

1. **Governance is the Road, Not the Speed Bump**: Sound risk controls and mathematical explainability do not slow innovation down—they make enterprise-scale deployment possible.
2. **Data Sovereignty & Privacy First**: Financial institutions must retain absolute ownership over customer data and proprietary model weights without third-party vendor lock-in.
3. **Resilience Under Stress**: Models must be evaluated not only for average accuracy during stable market regimes, but for deterministic safety during liquidity crises and systemic shocks.

## Research Collaborations

We actively collaborate with international academic institutions, central banks, and financial industry working groups to advance responsible AI adoption frameworks.
